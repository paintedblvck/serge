04 / About

Rwanda. Albany.
Everywhere.

Denis Kanaka is a Rwandan DJ, recording artist and creative strategist based in Albany, New York.

His work connects music, culture and storytelling, whether he is behind the decks at a lounge, in the studio on a new record, or shaping how a brand shows up in a room.

The thread through all of it is connection: bringing people together around sound and building nights they remember.
